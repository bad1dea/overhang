Overhang only changes pages on the front screen of iPhone Duo, where Safari keeps a column of buttons beside the page. On other iPhones Safari already gives pages the whole screen, and Overhang leaves them unchanged.

To try it on an iPhone Duo:
1. Open Overhang and tap Open Safari Settings (or go to Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions > Overhang).
2. Turn the extension on and set All Websites to Allow.
3. Open any site in Safari on the front screen. The page fills the screen, under Safari's buttons.
4. The round button in Safari's column, above New Tab, switches it off and on for that site. Overhang's toolbar menu has the same switches.

The extension makes no network requests. Its source is at https://github.com/bad1dea/overhang
